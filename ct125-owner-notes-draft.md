# CT125 owner notes · draft

From hondatrail125.com, in our words. Each claim links to its post. **→** says where it goes.

## Drain plug, not the tensioner bolt · JA55
**→ Engine oil guide**
- Two bolts under the engine. The drain plug faces straight down, right of centre. The forward-facing bolt, left of centre with a copper washer, is the cam chain tensioner sealing bolt. ([rkutzner](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5459/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5460/))
- Removing it drops the tensioner spring and plunger; the tensioner then needs re-priming. ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5461/))
- A spring lost inside is reached through the left (alternator) cover, possibly with the rotor off. ([inroads](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24574/), [m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24581/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24615/))
- JA65: one 12 mm bolt underneath; the tensioner bolt moved and is now an Allen bolt. ([Dr. Spark](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24618/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24619/))

Manual: drain bolt 24 N·m; tensioner sealing bolt 22 N·m (JA55 1-12).

## Check the oil on delivery · both
**→ Engine oil guide**
- Several new bikes arrived low, one below the dipstick. ([cyb3rbyte](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19874/), [brbo](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/18776/))
- JA65: 0.8 L read low for some; 900–950 mL reached the upper mark. ([rywil101](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/18771/), [RayWebb1](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/18832/))

Manual: JA55 0.7 L after draining; JA65 0.8 L, 0.85 L with filter (1-7). Dipstick rests on the threads (already in the guide).

## Early first change, magnetic plug · both
**→ Engine oil guide**
- Changes at 50–300 mi turned up metal flakes; magnetic plugs kept collecting through ~900 mi. ([Fun4me](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/3317/), [AstroStrat89](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/3435/), [AstroStrat89](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4383/))
- Against: early changes don't help break-in. ([NMCoyote](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19923/))
- Gold Plug MP-01. Thread M12×1.5, same on JA65; OEM plug 90131-883-000. ([Fun4me](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/3579/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19362/))
- To swap the plug without draining, lay the bike on its left side. ([TrailBoss](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/23479/))
- Check a generic magnetic plug against stock: a long magnet can reach the strainer screen. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24880/))

Manual: first change at 600 mi (schedule).

## Oil · both
**→ Engine oil guide**
- Use motorcycle oil: the clutch runs in it, and car oils' friction modifiers can make it slip. ([Dover_MA](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/3983/))
- Owners run GN4, HP4S, Rotella T4/T6, Mobil 1 4T, Motul 5100, Castrol 4T. GN4 is hard to find away from dealers. ([SneakyDingo](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/23380/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/32396/))
- Some change at half the interval: shifting turns notchy around 1,800 mi. ([SneakyDingo](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/23234/))

Manual: 10W-30, JASO MA; API SG+ (JA55), SJ+ (JA65).

## Drain plug threads and washer · both
**→ Engine oil guide**
- A JA65 drain boss stripped: plug seated hard with a socket, then torqued. ([Fishwishin](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27086/))
- Repair: oversize drain plug, or a thread insert (Time-Sert over Helicoil; the boss is shallow). ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27098/), [m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27101/))
- Torque assumes clean threads and a new washer. ([sieg](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27099/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27114/))
- New crush washer each change. One leak stopped after flipping it. ([SneakyDingo](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24867/), [Low-gear](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/24844/))

## Oil filter · JA65
**→ Engine oil filter guide (JA65)**
- Filter 15412-MGS-D21 (also Grom, C125). O-ring 91302-K0G-911; the Grom's 91302-PA9-003 is the same size (39.8 × 2.2) at about 5× the price. ([BaldRider](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19025/))
- Cover bolts snug; the O-ring seals. Don't pinch it. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19095/), [BaldRider](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19097/))

## Strainer screen and centrifugal filter · JA55
**→ Strainer screen / centrifugal filter guides**
- The right cover gasket often tears: keep a spare. ([chuckklr98](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5613/), [m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5615/))
- Auto-clutch linkage falls out with the cover; lay the bike over to refit it. ([Migolito](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/1027/), [-Nate](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5628/))

## Pop or clunk when shifting · both
**→ Troubleshooting; Clutch system guide**
- Usually the auto clutch out of adjustment, often after the right cover was off. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/7267/), [woops](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/7301/), [Aaron SG](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/7355/))
- Adjuster: slotted screw with a 14 mm locknut, right side of the crankcase. Loosen the nut, screw in one turn, back out until resistance, then in ⅛–¼ turn; hold it and lock the nut. ([Dakar Dan](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4453/))
- Don't shift with the engine off and the wheel still. ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/7269/))

## Gear change pedal recall · JA55
**→ Troubleshooting**
- Weak weld; recall for 2021 frames JA55-1000004 to JA55-1020651, later issued in the US and NZ. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5276/), [Rider](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5357/))
- Common mod: cut off the heel, move the pedal up a spline. ([FleaMike](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5277/), [FleaMike](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5344/))

## Valve clearance · both
**→ Valve clearance guide**
- JA55 exhausts are often 0.15 mm (0.006″): in spec, tight end. ([Migolito](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/2157/), [Fun4me](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/17419/))
- JA65 specs differ; 0.15 mm is out of spec there. ([NMCoyote](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/27235/))
- Plug out; turn the engine with a 17 mm socket through the large left-cover plug; marks under the small one. ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19230/), [m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/19243/))

Manual: JA55 IN 0.10, EX 0.17 ± 0.02 mm; JA65 IN 0.08, EX 0.20 ± 0.02 mm.

## Chain · both
**→ Drive chain guide**
- Stock chain is unsealed. Owners fit a DID 428 VX X-ring cut to 108 links. ([STUBBORN](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/11100/), [STUBBORN](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/12154/))
- Slack is measured on the centre stand. Often loose from the dealer. ([Almostcleavername](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4271/), [FRAC](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4274/))

Manual: 25–35 mm, limit 50 mm; DID428HDS3-108RB.

## Flat with no puncture · both
**→ Troubleshooting; Wheels/tires guide**
- A misaligned air chuck bent the valve; it leaked slowly, the tube slipped and tore at the valve. ([Gene56](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/12456/))
- Pressures are also on a sticker on the chain guard. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/12144/))

Manual: 25 psi front, 33 psi rear.

## Rear brake squeak · both
**→ Troubleshooting; Brake pads guide**
- Glazed pads: clean, scuff on emery cloth. ([Rackbid](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/9020/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/8974/))
- Same pad part numbers JA55 and JA65; EBC FA375HH fit. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/9520/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/23186/))

## Flashing ABS light · ABS models
**→ Troubleshooting**
- Most often low battery voltage; charging cleared it. ([Kev250R](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/15322/), [trailrider305](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/15332/))
- Front sensor behind the ABS label on the right fork leg; the other reads the countershaft. Tyre or sprocket changes can set a code. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/15306/))

Manual: ABS DTC index (JA55 4-29, JA65 4-55); DTC 7-1 tyre size / sprocket ratio (JA55 4-40).

## Starter won't crank · both
**→ Troubleshooting**
- Battery first, then the relay connections and the starter button, which corrodes on bikes kept outside. ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/20505/), [oldskool](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/20510/))
- Suspect added accessories: one USB charger was the drain. ([MJ 125](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/20491/))

Manual: starter troubleshooting (JA55 4-26, JA65 4-51).

## Battery storage · both
**→ Troubleshooting**
- A maintainer, not a plain trickle charger. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5366/))
- An SAE lead routed under the seat saves removing panels. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/5667/))

## Wobble at speed · both
**→ Troubleshooting**
- Check tyre pressure; luggage, hand guards and loose clothing change it. ([m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4489/), [Rider](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/4490/))

## Crankcase breather · both
**→ Crankcase breather guide**
- Tube from behind the starter to the airbox; no PCV valve. Leave it connected. ([dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/9783/), [dmonkey](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/9787/), [m in sc](https://www.hondatrail125.com/index.php?posts/9794/))
